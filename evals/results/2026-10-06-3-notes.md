## What changed (content tuning)

1. **Drop reasons in the results JSON** (`evals/score.ts` `dropReasons`, shown in the report): every call's raw answer
   replayed through the app's `validateDraft` with the same request plan (`prompt.ts` `planRequest`), refills included.
   On the run before it showed: 6 of the 7 incomplete passes were on Klyde Warren, Spring Creek Forest and Connemara,
   lost to generic plant clues (only "fruit or seeds") and to repeated ids (Klyde Warren's answers used one warbler 4
   times); the 7th was a timeout.
2. **Spares scaled to the pool** (`sparesFor`): a low-data pool (fewer describable items than n + 2) asks for 1 spare,
   other pools for exactly n. A Wild Find is describable only when its summary has a looks-like word outside its own
   names (`wild.ts` `looksOutsideNames`; also the pool order).
3. **The retry is a refill** (`refillPlan`, `build-pass.ts`): only the missing items (+1 spare), from items the first
   answer did not fill, without items whose clue failed a content check when the rest can still fill, and with a note
   naming the copied phrases and generic clues. If the first answer kept nothing, the retry is the whole request.
4. **Clue variety:** per-park first words (`openersFor`, 24-word bank); the "playful dares" voice is gone; the prompt
   bans the stock openings and "a place with / a spot where", and asks not to copy 3+ words from the SOURCE.
5. **Word variants in the Park Finds facts** (`park.ts` `chooseWords`): `{a|b}` slots chosen per park by hash. Gemma
   copied a 4-word run of its fact sheet into about half of all Park Find clues before (135/292) and after the prompt
   rule (60/118 in a smoke), so the fix is in the source text, not the prompt.
6. **Style checks are preferences** (`validate.ts` `StyleReason`): copying a 4-word run of the own source, a stock or
   repeated opening, and (low data only) a near-repeat make an item the first to go when a spare can replace it; they
   never cost completeness. Hard drops are unchanged: safety, grounding, name leaks, numbers, counts, season, map.
7. **Low data, generic check:** a Wild Find clue passes when its describing word is in its own quote and that quote
   holds a looks-like word ("bright yellow" proved by "yellow garden spider"). A trait the quote does not hold stays a
   drop (the recorded refill's "a round shell" for a snail is dropped).
8. **Checks:** a clue cut off mid-sentence is dropped (`cut_off`); the generic check also matches by word ending
   ("wades" ~ "wading": a real heron clue dropped in the run before).
9. The no-AI template is exempt from the source-copy check only (it copies source sentences by design).

## What was tried and dropped

- **Short JSON keys** (`id`, `where`, `quote`): 58.1 answer tokens per item vs 57.6 before (smoke 0544), so reverted.
- **2 spares for low-data pools, 0 for pools with 2n describable items, 1 otherwise:** first calls asking for n+2 took
  12-20 s and still needed the refill (smokes 0544, 0552); replaced by 1 / 0.
- **Copying as a hard drop for Park Finds:** cost 2 of 17 passes their completeness in the 1-run smoke 0552 (Oak Point
  4/8, Breckinridge 5/8), and the refill copied again even with the exact phrases banned; now a preference.
- **Generic-check rescue whenever the quote has a looks-like word:** let "a round shell" through for a quote about an
  operculum; narrowed (see 7).
