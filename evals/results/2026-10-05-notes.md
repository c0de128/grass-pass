## What failed, and why
- **M3 complete passes: 64.7% (33/51 Gemma runs), threshold 90%.** Almost every lost item is a name-leak drop (below). A pass with 6 of 8 items still prints, but it is not "complete" by the SPEC definition (>= n-1).
- **M6 name leaks before filter: 17.5% (85/487), threshold 5%.** In the clue itself: 7.6% (37). The other 48 are in `lookWhere` only. Name leaks were the ONLY drop reason besides 4 ungrounded quotes. Kinds of leak seen:
  - real giveaways the filter is right to drop: "a small insect that makes honey" (Western Honey Bee, 9 times), "flowers shaped like trumpets" (trumpet vine), "a type of oak" (bur oak), "a red fungus that looks like a hollow cage" (red-cage fungus);
  - place words in `lookWhere` that are also the answer: "at the pond" for "Pond or lake" (10 times), "in a garden" for Yellow Garden Spider (8), "in the prairie" for Snow-on-the-prairie (4), "over the creek" for a bridge on Rowlett Creek Trail;
  - **false positives from OSM names (a bug for the S3 owner, `distinctiveWords` in `src/lib/pool/types.ts`)**: mapped names like "Sheila and Jody Grant Children's Park" put `and`, `the`, `park` into `nameWords`, so a clue with the word "and" is dropped. At least 8 Gemma drops are only `and` / `the` / `park`. Fixing that (skip stopwords and the word "park") would raise M3 and lower M6 without touching safety.
  - Possible prompt fix (S8): tell the model that `lookWhere` must not use words from the item's name either, and give one bad example.
- **M7 latency: Gemma p50 13.8 s / p95 24.3 s, thresholds 10 s / 20 s.** 61 of 63 calls took over 10 s (fastest 6.8 s, slowest 30.0 s = one timeout). Prompts were 645-4,007 tokens. This matches the S3 measurement (11-17 s); the 7-9 s in ADR 0001 was for a smaller 6-item prompt. Mitigation is S8's job (pre-warmed example parks, progress text), and the SPEC threshold should be revisited by the PM.
- **Llama 4 Maverick:** 4 of 20 runs timed out at the app's 60 s Llama setting (cases 1, 2 and 3 at the start of the run, then case 14); the others took 19-47 s. Its p50 is 28.8 s, far slower than Gemma, and its grounding/leak numbers are not better. It stays a slow fallback only.
- **No-AI template:** passes M3/M6 by construction (it copies one source sentence and masks the name), but reads at grade 5.9 (FAIL vs 3.5) and has no "look where" hints. That is the honest baseline the open model is compared with.
