## What this run says about the audit round 2 change

- **It fixed wrong counts.** 0 of 124 printed count clues are wrong, against 24 of 88 before. The check removed only 1
  Gemma item; the prompt's count rule did most of the work.
- **It cut repetition, but not enough.** M10 went from 45.7% to 29.2%. The 5-word runs most often shared are shared
  phrasing, not shared facts: "find a place with a" (8 parks), "can you find 2 spots" (5), "a place with a roof" (4),
  "makes a soft rushing sound" (4, the creek fact), "climb slide and swing on" (4, the playground fact). Part of it is
  the rotating facts themselves: when two parks get the same fact for a kind, Gemma often quotes its words. The 6-voice
  idea did not spread the openings ("Find a place with a ...", "Can you find ...") enough.
- **It cost completeness.** M3 fell from 100% to 86.3%. The 7 incomplete Gemma passes: Klyde Warren x 3 (5, 6, 6 of 8;
  pool 4 park / 6 wild), Spring Creek Forest x 2 (6 and 5 of 8; pool 2 / 7), Connemara run 2 (6 of 8; pool 1 / 11),
  and Breckinridge run 1 (the 30 s timeout). These are the parks with the least data. The new checks (generic plant
  clue, trait word from the source, near-repeat on the same pass) drop more of their clues, and with only 1 spare
  item a second call does not always refill them. The results file does not store each drop's reason, so the split
  between those checks was not measured here. 10 of 60 Gemma runs needed a second call.
- **It cost a little speed.** The median Gemma answer grew from 450 to 516 tokens (the spare item and longer clues), and
  p50 went from 9.4 s to 10.1 s. The p95 of 20.8 s is mostly the few slow provider calls listed above.
