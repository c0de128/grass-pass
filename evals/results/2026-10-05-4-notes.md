## What changed in the app between run 3 and this run
- **Season check (audit R1-M4):** the prompt now says the month ("Today is in October."), and every plant in the
  pool carries a code-written season note from iNaturalist "Flowers and Fruits" annotations: observations of that
  species within 50 km of the park, in this calendar month, all years (`src/lib/sources/inat-phenology.ts`). Flowers
  (or fruit/seeds) count as "seen" with at least 3 such records and at least 20% of that species' annotated records
  this month. A clue or hint about flowers, blooms, petals, fruit, berries, seeds, pods, nuts or acorns for a plant
  without that support is dropped (`out_of_season`). The 20 eval fixtures gained the three recorded phenology answers
  per park (nothing else in them changed). Live near Connemara: Callery pear 1 of 2 annotated October records show
  flowers (not supported); Maximilian sunflower 26 of 27 (supported).
- **Retry at n-1 (R1-m1):** one retry when fewer than n-1 items survive (was n-2). 3 Gemma passes used it.
- **Stricter name-leak check (R1-m3):** a word that starts with the stem of a 5+ letter name word is a leak
  ("Passifloraceae" for Passiflora, "Columbidae" for Columba, "diamond" for fields mapped as "... Diamonds"). The eval
  scorer uses the same function, so M6 is measured more strictly than in run 3.
- **No map, no "map" (R1-m4):** the eval passes have no Find This Spot map, so the prompt says so and a clue that says
  "map" is dropped (a hint is blanked).
- **Contact details (R1-m7):** bare domains, @handles and 7+ digit runs are blocked in printed text, and OSM names that
  contain them never reach the prompt. No eval case contains one.
- **Park Finds that make a child look closely (R1-m10):** for ages 6-10 and 10-13 the prompt asks for a count to check
  or a detail to look for, not "a place to sit".

## Honest caveats
- **M6 fails, and got worse (10.5%).** 47 of 447 Gemma items used a word of their own answer before the filter: 30 in
  the clue, 17 only in the hint. Causes, from reading all 47:
  - the new look-closely rule produced count clues that name the thing: "Count the bridges. Can you find all 8?" (x3),
    "Count the ponds.", "Count the baseball fields.";
  - the stricter check: "Find a dirt diamond" for "Celebration Park Baseball Diamonds" (x3, the generic noun is in the
    OSM name), "a member of the bird family Columbidae" for a rock pigeon;
  - the rest are the usual ones: "by the stream" for a creek, "climbing on other plants" for climbing hempvine,
    "a type of oak" for bur oak, "flowers that look like trumpets" for trumpet vine.
  Code drops every leaky clue and blanks every leaky hint, so nothing is given away on a printed pass, and every
  data-rich Gemma pass still kept at least n-1 finds. Proposed next step (not done in this round, it needs a new
  eval run): tell the model a count clue must not name the thing ("Count the hoops", never "Count the bridges"),
  and give the stem rule an exception for a feature's own generic noun when it is in an OSM name.
- **Llama 4 Maverick got much worse:** 80.1% grounded (was 94.3%) and 41.2% complete passes (was 82.4%). 22 of its 36
  ungrounded quotes were the code-written season note copied as the quote ("flowers seen in October"), even though
  the prompt says not to; Gemma did this once while the prompt was being written and not at all after the "never
  copy it" line. Llama also needed 12 retries, and many of its two-call passes hit the 85 s pass deadline. Llama is
  only the slow fallback. A likely fix: move the season fact into the plant's source text as a code-written sentence
  (quotable and true, like the OpenStreetMap counts in Park Finds) instead of a tag attribute. Not done here.
- **The season rule is cautious.** A plant with fewer than 3 annotated records this month is treated as not
  flowering: autumn clematis near White Rock Lake (2 of 3 October records show flowers) is told "no flowers", so the
  model writes about its leaves. That costs variety, never correctness.
- **Phenology costs 3 more iNaturalist calls** for a park's first pass (about 3 s at our 1 request per second), then
  it is cached for 7 days. If iNaturalist does not answer within 8 s, no plant's flowers or fruit count as in season.
- Find This Spot is still not in the eval (no recorded geometry). The glued-field glitch from run 2 did not happen
  (0 of 447 Gemma quotes).
